Overview

This UE5 Souls-like game project focused on environment art and level design, including set dressing, prop and character design, level layout and scene rendering. The aim was to organise orientation, progression and combat encounters within an explorable environment.

Guide action through the environment

Spatial layouts, landmarks and combat areas guide movement and connect routes through the level. The environment establishes atmosphere while communicating direction and possibilities for action.

My contribution

From October to November 2025, I worked as a level artist and designer. I produced and integrated assets and materials using tools including ZBrush, 3ds Max, Substance 3D Painter and RealityScan, then assembled and rendered the environment in UE5.
