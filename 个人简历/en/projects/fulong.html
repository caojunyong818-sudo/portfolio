Overview

This cultural revitalisation project for Fulong New Street in Macao was developed through collaboration between Harbin Institute of Technology and Macau University of Science and Technology. The team explored how spatial design and interactive experiences could make neighbourhood stories more discoverable and understandable.

Place cultural clues within exploration

The interactive component uses geographical exploration and clue collection to turn the team’s cultural research into lightweight tasks. Finding, triggering and receiving feedback gradually introduce information about the street, connecting narrative to action.

My contribution: from team proposal to playable experience

I was primarily responsible for game programming, using UE5 Blueprints to turn the team’s research, story and interaction concepts into a playable mini-game. I organised task flows and triggers so that cultural content could emerge during play.

The video and project booklet below document the team’s field research, open-design strategies and spatial proposals, providing the wider context for the interactive prototype.
