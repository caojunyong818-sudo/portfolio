Overview

This collection brings together animation, lighting and rendering exercises featuring a space station, ruins, desert, snow and stylised environments. It explores how light, composition and camera movement organise space and reveal atmosphere over time.

Train visual judgement across environments

Exterior scenes focus on weather, light and spatial depth. Ruin and space scenes explore atmosphere and focal points, while stylised studies examine relationships between colour and form. Animation and camera exercises test how a static composition develops over time.

From composition to motion

The films can be viewed with attention to how light directs the eye and how camera movement reveals space. They are grouped by exercise so that different approaches can be compared.
