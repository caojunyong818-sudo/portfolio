Overview

These Processing and p5.js coursework experiments combine generated graphics, mouse interaction, camera input and sound into real-time visual systems. They explore how input is read, transformed and returned as visual feedback, and how a small set of rules can produce continuous variation.

From visual rules to live input

01

Particle fields

Use vector fields to control particles, exploring nebula vortices and breathing grids. Parameters and palettes reveal changes in trajectory density and rhythm.

02

Camera signals

Transform image brightness into scan lines, combining RGB offsets, contours and trails. Line spacing, intensity and width become adjustable visual variables.

03

Sound and ripples

Translate camera motion and microphone input into ripples, refraction and movement, exploring relationships between the body, sound and images.

Method and attribution

Repeated changes to rules and parameters, compared against the resulting images, helped me understand direction, form and visual rhythm. The screenshots also include basic mouse-condition exercises; linked webpages provide access to the live experiments.

The particle-field exercise adapts an OpenProcessing example by Vamoss. The original link and attribution remain in the source code. It is presented here as a learning and extension exercise.
