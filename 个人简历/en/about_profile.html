Creative Interests

My interest in games and digital art grew through years of playing games, watching films and engaging with interactive media. I see games as an art form in which image, sound, rules, space and player behaviour work together.

Souls-like level design, environmental storytelling in open worlds and social interaction in cooperative games have shown me how digital spaces can carry complex emotions, cultures and relationships. My work invites audiences to enter through action, exploration and feedback.
